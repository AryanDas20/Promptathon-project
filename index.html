HOW VAULT WORKS (HIGH SCHOOL ARCHITECTURE GUIDE)

Distributed systems sound complex, but they are built on simple real-world ideas! Here is how Vault keeps your data 100% safe:

1. Object Sharding (Tearing the Book)

Instead of saving a huge 100GB video as a single massive file, Vault tears it into smaller 64MB chapters called Chunks. This allows multiple storage servers to download and read pieces simultaneously.

2. Quorum Replication (N=3 Safety Copies)

Every chunk is stored on 3 different computers across distinct data centers. Even if 2 computers catch fire or lose power, your data remains accessible from the 3rd replica.

3. Consistent Hash Ring (Clock Mapping)

Imagine a 360-degree clock. Every server gets a position on the clock. When a new file chunk is saved, its fingerprint maps to a number on the clock, placing it in the nearest server.

4. Bit-Rot Detection & Merkle Trees

Over time, physical computer hard drives age and flip random 0s and 1s (Bit-Rot). Vault checks SHA-256 digital fingerprints every few seconds to catch and repair silent corruption automatically.
